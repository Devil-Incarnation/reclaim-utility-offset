— HOW IT WORKS

From usage to receipt in minutes.

Reclaim Protocol connects real-world electricity usage to a verifiable carbon receipt through a simple, transparent process.

01
📄

Enter your usage

Provide your electricity provider, billing period and consumption details.

→
02
🛡️

Verify the source

Reclaim uses zkTLS to verify that the data comes from a trusted source (e.g. your utility portal).

→
03
📊

Calculate CO₂e

We apply a documented emissions factor to convert your electricity usage (kWh) into estimated CO₂e.

→
04
🌿

Link a registry record

Your receipt references a public carbon-credit registry (e.g. Verra or Gold Standard).

→
05
📦

Get your receipt

We record the receipt on a public blockchain (testnet) and generate a shareable, QR-coded receipt.
